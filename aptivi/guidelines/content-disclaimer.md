We strive to always create content with human intervention. However, there are cases where we may use such technology mentioned above to assist in creating content or its parts, such as lines of code or documentation. In those cases, you may see indications that AI has been used as part of this content. Those indicators are found in different places, whether inside the image, at the bottom of the image, or somewhere else. The agent or the app used will be mentioned explicitly to let people know that we used AI to generate content in some situations.

**To put it short, all our content is made by humans, unless otherwise indicated.**
